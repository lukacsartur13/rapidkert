A Győr team, one responsibility.

Rapidkert builds gardens and irrigation systems in Győr and the villages around it. We work the way we do — one team, from the plan to the handover — because most of the delay and most of the wasted money we have seen comes from one person planning, another crew paving and somebody else again fitting the irrigation. 03 The order Bottom up

A garden is built from the bottom up.

A

Why one team

Every layer goes underneath the next one. If the paving is finished before the irrigation pipework, it has to be opened up. If the lawn goes down before the drainage, it has to be opened up again. That is not an organisational question — it is the physics of the work.

With a separate planner, a separate paving crew and a separate irrigation fitter, somebody would have to coordinate that order. Here there is nobody to coordinate: the same team lays all of it, in the same order.

05 The process What a job looks like with us

Clear stages, a realistic schedule.

At every stage you know where we are and what comes next.

01 — Survey

Site survey

We come out, look at the ground, the soil and the orientation, and measure the pressure at the water source. Free, with no obligation.

02 — Planning

Plan and quote

We set out the garden's layout and the irrigation zoning, then give an itemised, fixed-price quote.

03 — Groundwork

Groundwork

Grading, levelling, foundations, drainage. What nobody sees, and what decides everything.

04 — Construction

Construction

Paving, irrigation, planting, lawn – in a coordinated sequence, so nothing has to be opened up twice.

05 — Handover

Handover

We commission it, show you how it runs, and hand it over. We can look after the garden afterwards too.

06 Where we work Győr and the surrounding area
Where we work

Győr and a 40 km radius around it. Further afield for larger projects.

A local team — and that is not a figure of speech.

Most of our work is in Győr and its immediate area: Győrújbarát, Győrújfalu, Abda, Nyúl, Écs, Pannonhalma, Töltéstava, Kisbajcs, Vámosszabadi, Gyirmót and Ménfőcsanak. Which means that if something goes wrong after handover, we are not there in a fortnight's time. For larger projects we also travel out towards Mosonmagyaróvár, Csorna, Kapuvár and Pápa.
